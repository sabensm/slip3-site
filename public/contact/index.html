01 Game support

For help with a Slip3 game, email slip3studios@gmail.com. Include the game’s name, your device and iOS version, and what happened. A screenshot or screen recording helps.

Purchases are handled by Apple. To request a refund, use Report a Problem on Apple’s site.

02 Privacy requests

To access, correct or delete your data in a Slip3 game, or to exercise any right in its privacy policy, email slip3studios@gmail.com with “Privacy request” in the subject and the game’s name.

Each game has its own privacy policy and terms, linked above. The website has its own privacy page.
