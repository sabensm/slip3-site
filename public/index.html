02 How we build

  1. One job, done well.

    Each app does a single thing people already do — call a play, play a daily game, keep a recipe — and tries to do it better.

  2. Respect the minute.

    Fast to the good part. A play in sixty seconds. A game in about one.

  3. Ship, then sharpen.

    Small releases, real feedback, steady refinement across iPhone and the web.
