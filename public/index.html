03 How we build

  1. One job, done well.

    Each app covers one thing people already do: calling a play, playing a daily game, keeping the family recipes.

  2. Respect the minute.

    A play drawn in sixty seconds. A daily game in about one.

  3. Ship, then sharpen.

    Small releases, often. The Starting 5 is on release 2.8.
