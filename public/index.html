How we build

Slip3 Studios designs, builds and ships apps for iPhone, iPad and the web, and keeps refining each one long after launch.

  1. Start with an idea.

    Every project begins with something we want to exist. We shape it just far enough to put it in someone’s hands.

  2. Test it.

    Early builds go to real players on TestFlight or the web, well before they feel finished. We watch how people use them and listen to what they say, and that sets the direction.

  3. Keep iterating.

    Launch is one step in the loop. We keep shipping small updates for as long as people are playing, each one shaped by what the last one taught us.
