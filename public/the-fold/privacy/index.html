Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to The Fold, the recipe app for iPhone, iPad and Mac, and its share extension (together, “the app”), made by Slip3 Studios (“we”). The Fold is built to keep your recipes private. They are shared only with the groups you create or join, never with the public.

The short version

  • We store your account, recipes and photos so they sync across your devices and reach the groups you share with.
  • We don’t sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.
  • You can delete your account, and everything in it, from inside the app at any time.

Information we collect

  • Account information. You sign in with Apple. We receive a unique identifier for your Apple ID and, if you choose to share it, your email address (which may be an Apple private relay address). We also store the display name and optional profile photo you set.
  • Your recipes. Recipes you create, scan or import, including titles, ingredients, steps, notes, photos, and the source link of a recipe you saved from the web.
  • Groups. The groups you create or join, who belongs to them, and which recipes are shared with each one.
  • Diagnostics and usage. If you’ve agreed to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple provides us with crash reports and aggregate usage statistics. Crash reports are not linked to your identity.

What stays on your device

  • Scanning. When you scan a handwritten recipe card, the text is read on your device with Apple’s Vision framework. The camera image isn’t sent to us unless you save it as a recipe photo.
  • Web import. When you save a recipe from a website, your device fetches the page directly to read the recipe. We receive only the recipe you save.
  • Camera and photos. The app asks for camera and photo library access only to scan recipes and attach photos you pick.

How we use your information

We use your information only to run the app: to sign you in, store and sync your recipes, share them with the members of your groups, show your name and photo to those members, and respond when you contact us. We never use it for advertising, and we never sell it.

Who can see your recipes

Your recipes are private to you until you share them with a group. Members of a group can see the recipes shared with it, along with your display name and profile photo. There are no public profiles or feeds.

Where your data is stored

Your account, recipes and photos are stored with Convex, the cloud database that powers syncing, on servers in the United States. Sign in is handled by Apple. These providers process data on our behalf and don’t use it for their own purposes. We don’t share your data with anyone else, except where required by law, such as in response to a valid legal request.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app under Settings › Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, your recipes and their photos, your group memberships, and any groups you own (including the recipes shared in them). Deletion can’t be undone. Recipes you shared to a group owned by someone else are removed from that group too.

You can also ask us to delete your data, or for a copy of it, by emailing slip3studios@gmail.com.

Data retention

We keep your data for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your data is removed from our database right away.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR in the EU and UK and the CCPA/CPRA in California, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, and to object to or restrict how it’s processed. We don’t sell or share personal information for advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of. To exercise any of these rights, email slip3studios@gmail.com. We won’t treat you differently for doing so.

International transfers

Your data is stored in the United States. If you use the app from elsewhere, your data is transferred there, with safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses where the law requires them.

Children

The Fold is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit between the app and our servers, and access to the database is limited to the studio. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information, and we’ll notify you of a breach that affects your data as the law requires.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll post the new version here with a new effective date, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.

Contact

Questions about privacy in The Fold: slip3studios@gmail.com, or see the contact page.

Effective November 13, 2026.
