The Fold

A private recipe vault for families. Keep the recipes that matter, and share them only with the people you invite.

01 The brief

Family recipes live on index cards, in group texts and in bookmarks that disappear. Most recipe apps fix that by publishing everything.

The Fold starts from the opposite assumption: a grandmother’s recipe card is not content. It belongs to the family, and the app should treat it that way.

So the core of the app is the group: a private vault that only invited members can see. Everything else, from scanning to cooking, is there to get recipes into that vault and onto the stove.

02 Key decisions

  1. Private by design.

    Groups are invite-only recipe vaults. Family and friends join with an invite code, and nothing is shared beyond the group.

  2. Bring the box of cards.

    Scan handwritten recipe cards into searchable recipes, and save from any site with one-tap web import from the share sheet.

  3. Made for the kitchen.

    Cook Mode sets bigger text, keeps the screen on, and steps through the recipe by voice, so nobody has to touch the screen with flour on their hands.

  4. Warm, not clinical.

    The design brief was “the digital kitchen table”: an oatmeal background, serif headings, terracotta accents and soft recipe cards instead of default lists.

03 In the App Store

04 How it’s built

The Fold is a native SwiftUI app for iPhone, iPad and Mac, written in Swift 6.2. Groups sync in real time, so a recipe saved at one kitchen table appears at the next without a refresh.

Sign in with Apple is the only account system. A share extension brings recipes in from Safari and other apps without opening The Fold first.
