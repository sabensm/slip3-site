A private recipe vault for families. Keep the recipes that matter, and share them only with the people you invite.

The idea

Family recipes live on index cards, in group texts and in bookmarks that disappear. Most recipe apps fix that by publishing everything.

The Fold starts from the opposite assumption. A grandmother’s recipe card belongs to the family, and the app treats it that way.

What’s inside

  1. Private by design.

    Groups are invite-only recipe vaults. Family and friends join with an invite code.

  2. Bring the box of cards.

    Scan handwritten recipe cards into searchable recipes, or save one from any site with a tap.

  3. Made for the kitchen.

    Cook Mode sets bigger text, keeps the screen on, and steps through the recipe by voice.
