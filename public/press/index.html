01 About the studio

Short

Slip3 Studios is an independent studio that designs, builds and ships apps for iPhone, iPad and the web.

Long

Slip3 Studios is an independent studio that designs, builds and ships apps for iPhone, iPad and the web. Founded by Mike Sabens, who has built consumer products at scale since 2008, the studio makes every app end to end and keeps refining them long after launch.

Its apps include Formation Lab, a play designer for youth flag football coaches; The Starting 5, a daily NBA trivia game; The Fold, a private recipe vault for families; and Plonk, a physics merge puzzle coming soon to iPhone.
