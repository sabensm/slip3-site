About Slip3

Hi, I'm Mike Sabens.

I design and ship small, focused games and apps for iPhone and the web, and I publish them under Slip3 Studios.

I've been in tech since 2008 — nearly twenty years, from project manager to leading product at scale, building consumer products people already use. I never got a computer science degree. About ten years ago I hand-coded and shipped my first iOS app, a subscription tracker. The in-app purchase didn't sell. After I moved to Florida and started a new chapter, I stepped back from building.

I came back when AI made shipping practical again. Slip3 is that return: a studio I run myself, making the apps I wish existed. Formation Lab, Starting 5 Trivia, and The Fold are out now, with more on the way, including Plonk.

If you'd like to say hello, you can find me on X and LinkedIn. The work itself is on the studio home.
