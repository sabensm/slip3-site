About Slip3

Mike Sabens founded Slip3 Studios to build apps, games and experiences crafted with intention.

Since 2008, he has built consumer products at scale, including a top-ranked national sports app. At Slip3, he brings that same discipline to work that is entirely his own: he designs, builds, and ships every app himself.

He builds from what inspires him, and always puts in the time to make sure each project reflects a high standard of quality and craftsmanship.
