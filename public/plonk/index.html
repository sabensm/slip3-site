The idea

Every set has its own ladder of pieces to grow.

Critters runs from a ladybug to an elephant, Orchard from a blueberry to a watermelon, and League from a golf ball to a beach ball. Seasonal sets join for the holidays.

What’s inside

  1. Aim and drop.

    Move to aim, tap to drop. The Next preview shows what’s coming, so you can plan each drop.

  2. Match and grow.

    Two matching pieces combine into the next, bigger piece. Merge back to back to build combos.

  3. Real physics.

    Pieces fall, roll and settle. Every drop changes the pile, and the bin keeps filling.
