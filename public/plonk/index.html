Plonk: Merge Puzzle

Drop, Match, Stack & Grow

A cozy physics puzzle for a relaxing minute, or a long chase for a new best.

Play a quick round See the App Store art

Game Center leaderboardsiCloud stats sync

01 How a round goes

  1. Aim and drop.

    Move to aim, tap to drop. The Next preview shows what’s coming, so you can plan each drop.

  2. Match and grow.

    Two matching pieces combine into the next, bigger piece. Merge back to back to build combos.

  3. Real physics.

    Pieces fall, roll and settle. Every drop changes the pile, and the bin keeps filling.

Every set has its own ladder of pieces. Critters runs from a ladybug all the way up to an elephant, Orchard from a blueberry to a watermelon, and League from a golf ball to a beach ball. Seasonal sets join for the holidays, starting with a Thanksgiving harvest.
