The idea

A driving range with no end to it.

Every shot lands a little farther than the last. The course runs through Summer, Autumn, Blossom, Desert and Snow, then off the edge of the Earth to the Moon, and each world has its own skies, hazards and wildlife.

What’s inside

  1. One-tap swing.

    Tap to stop the power meter, tap again to aim. Hit the center for a pure strike.

  2. Upgrade and go farther.

    Every shot pays coins. Spend them on Power, Bounce and Roll, and reach the Moon’s far side to Prestige.

  3. A sillier roster.

    Start as a rookie and hire a pirate, a shark or a hot dog. A new club unlocks every level, and chests hold new balls.
