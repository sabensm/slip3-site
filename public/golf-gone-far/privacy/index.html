Golf Gone Far · Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what the Golf Gone Far app for iPhone and iPad (“Golf Gone Far”) collects, why, and who it goes to. Golf Gone Far is made by Slip3 Studios (“Slip3”, “we”).

What Golf Gone Far doesn't collect

Golf Gone Far has no accounts and no sign-up. The app doesn't ask for or collect your name, email address or any other contact details, and it doesn't send usage analytics to Slip3.

Information Collection and Use

Ads (Google AdMob)

Golf Gone Far shows ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Rewarded ads are optional: you can watch one to double a shot's coins or to boost your next few shots. If you haven't bought Remove Ads, interstitial ads show between shots.

  • Google may collect device identifiers, your IP address, approximate location derived from your IP address, how you interact with ads, and diagnostics. Google handles this under its own privacy policy; see also how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • The advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you allow tracking in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking (Apple's guide to tracking controls).
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your consent first. You can change that choice in Golf Gone Far's Settings → Ad Privacy Choices.
  • Buying Remove Ads stops interstitial ads. Rewarded ads stay available if you choose to watch them.

Purchases (RevenueCat and Apple)

In-app purchases are paid through Apple. Golf Gone Far uses RevenueCat to check what you've bought. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, purchase and receipt information, and device and app information. Slip3 never sees your payment details.

iCloud (Apple)

Your progress, unlocks and purchases (not your settings) are synced through your own iCloud key-value storage so your other devices match. Slip3 can't access it.

Cookies and tracking technologies

Golf Gone Far doesn't use cookies. The Google Mobile Ads SDK uses device identifiers and similar technologies as described above. The advertising identifier is used only with your permission through App Tracking Transparency, and in the EEA, UK and Switzerland only after the consent form.

Your Rights

You may ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we hold, and you may withdraw consent where we rely on it. Golf Gone Far doesn't send Slip3 data that identifies you, so most requests are best made to Google, RevenueCat or Apple, who hold the data described above; we will help where we can. You can change ad consent in Settings → Ad Privacy Choices or in iOS tracking settings. To make a request, email support@slip3studios.com.

Your California privacy rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you live in California, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, to delete it, to correct it, to opt out of its sale or sharing, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights. Slip3 doesn't sell personal information for money. If you allow tracking, Google may use your device's advertising identifier for personalised ads, which California law may treat as “sharing”. You can opt out by not allowing tracking, or by turning it off in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. To use your other rights, email support@slip3studios.com.

Third Party Access

Golf Gone Far's data goes only to the services described above, each under its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob (ads)
  • RevenueCat (purchases)
  • Apple (payments, iCloud)

We may also disclose information:

  • as required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena or similar legal process;
  • when we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request.

International Data Transfers

Google, RevenueCat and Apple may process data in the United States and other countries outside your own, including outside the European Economic Area. Where the law requires safeguards for these transfers, they rely on mechanisms such as:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission
  • Adequacy decisions or other legally recognized transfer mechanisms
  • Your consent, where required and legally permitted

Opt-Out Rights

  • Ad tracking: don't allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt, or turn it off in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Ad consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): Settings → Ad Privacy Choices.
  • Everything: uninstall Golf Gone Far. This stops all collection, but it doesn't delete data already sent to the services above.

Data Retention Policy

  • Ads, purchases and iCloud data: kept by Google, RevenueCat and Apple under their own policies.
  • Data on your device stays until you delete Golf Gone Far. Data in your iCloud stays until you remove it.
  • Data we must keep by law: kept as long as the law requires.

To ask us to delete data, email support@slip3studios.com.

Children

Golf Gone Far is a general-audience app. It isn't intended for children under 13, or such higher age as applicable law requires, and it isn't in the App Store's Kids category. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you're a parent or guardian and believe your child has given us personal information, email support@slip3studios.com and we will delete it.

Security

Golf Gone Far doesn't send data to Slip3's own servers. Ads, purchases and iCloud are handled by Google, RevenueCat and Apple, each with its own security measures.

Data Breach Notification

If a data breach affects your personal data, we will notify you as applicable law requires, including, where required, what happened and what we are doing about it.

Changes

We may update this policy, for example when Golf Gone Far changes. We will post the updated policy here with a new effective date. Where the law requires it, we will ask for your consent to material changes before they take effect. Previous versions are available on request from support@slip3studios.com.

This privacy policy is effective as of 2026-11-19.

Your Consent

Where we rely on consent (ad tracking through App Tracking Transparency, and ad consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), you can withdraw it at any time using the controls above. Withdrawing doesn't affect processing that took place before.

Contact Us

For questions about privacy in Golf Gone Far, email support@slip3studios.com.
