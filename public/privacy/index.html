Website privacy

This page covers the website at slip3studios.com. Each app has its own privacy policy, linked below.

What this site collects

  • No cookies, no ads, no tracking across sites. The site sets no cookies and loads no advertising or social-media trackers.
  • Aggregate page views. The site uses Vercel Web Analytics, which counts page views and referrers without cookies and without identifying you. It doesn’t build a profile of you or follow you to other sites.
  • Server logs. The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard request logs (such as IP address and browser type) to run and secure the service.
  • Your theme choice. If you switch between light and dark mode, that choice is saved in your own browser’s local storage. It never leaves your device.

Fonts and media

Fonts, images and video are served from this site itself. Your browser doesn’t contact a third-party font service when you visit.

Email

If you email the studio, your message and address are used only to reply to you or to send what you asked for. They are never sold or shared.

Links to other sites

Links to the App Store, the apps’ own websites, X and LinkedIn are governed by those services’ policies.

  • The Fold
  • Formation Lab and The Starting 5: see each app’s App Store listing and website.

Contact
