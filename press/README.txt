ABOUT
Slip3 Studios is an independent studio that designs, builds and ships apps for
iPhone, iPad and the web. Founded by Mike Sabens, the studio makes Formation Lab,
a play designer for flag football coaches; The Starting 5, a daily NBA
trivia game; The Fold, a private recipe vault for families; Plonk, a physics
merge puzzle for iPhone; and Golf Gone Far, a one-tap distance golf game for
iPhone and iPad.
